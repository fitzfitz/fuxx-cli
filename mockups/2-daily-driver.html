What you're looking at

  1. Scrollback and scrollP0M5 Scroll back through past output with the trackpad or wheel. The scrollbar marks where search hits are.
  2. Selection, copy and paste, mouseP1M5 Drag to select, copy, paste. Mouse events go to programs that ask for them (vim, htop).
  3. Clickable URLs and pathsP1M5 Links and file:line paths are detected and open on click.
  4. Robust resizeM5 Resizing reflows cleanly; the new grid size (columns × rows) flashes while you drag.
  5. Font family, size, ligaturesP1M6 Your own font. Ligatures turn =>, ->, >= and != into single glyphs.
  6. Theme from your Ghostty configP2M6 Colors, font and theme are read from Ghostty's config, like cmux does, so fuxx matches your terminal.
  7. Search in scrollbackP2milestone not yet assigned Find text in past output, step through matches. In the Features catalog; the roadmap hasn't placed it yet.
  8. Rename and reorder sessionsP1M7 Double-click to rename, drag the handle to reorder (the “invoice tests” row is doing both). Hand-set names win over program titles.
  9. Agent presetsP1M7 Start claude, opencode or codex in one action, in a chosen working folder.
  10. Session restoreP2M7 Quit and relaunch: your projects and their sessions come back with their names, order and working folders.
  11. Notification historyP2milestone not yet assigned An activity feed of what finished or asked for you, and when. Click an entry to go to that session.
  12. Tabs, or the sidebar as the switcherP1open question The catalog lists “tabs (or the sidebar list is the primary switcher)”. Shown here as sidebar-only; the choice is still open.
  13. Project groupsP1M7 Sessions grouped under their project (a named folder). Each header shows its most urgent ring (waiting, then done, then working), so a collapsed project still tells you it needs you. + starts a session in that project. You pick the project when you create a session; fuxx doesn't follow a shell's cd.

Several overlays show at once here so every feature is visible in one picture. In the real app you'd see one at a time.
