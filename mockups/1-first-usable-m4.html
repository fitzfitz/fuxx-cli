What you're looking at

  1. A real GPU terminalP0M1 Full VT screen from alacritty_terminal, drawn with GPUI's text system: 16 colors, truecolor, bold, dim, italic, underline, inverse and a block cursor. Menlo 14.
  2. Runs any program, keyboard, resizeP0M1 Your login shell on a PTY (portable-pty). Keys become terminal bytes; resizing the window resizes the PTY. vim, htop and agents work.
  3. Session sidebar and quick-switchP0M3 220 px list, one row per session. ⌘T new · ⌘W close · ⌘1…9 jump · ⌘{ / ⌘} previous / next. Click a row to switch.
  4. Labels come from the programP1M3 A row shows the title the program sets (OSC 0/2), else “Shell N”. Renaming by hand comes in M7.
  5. Window title follows the active sessionM3 “fuxx — <label>”. Closing the last session closes the window.
  6. Status ring per sessionP0M4 Hollow grey = Idle · amber = Working · red = Waiting (needs you) · green = Done. Updates live, for every session at once.
  7. Agent-state detectionP0M2 Output means Working; a prompt like “(y/n)” in the last lines means Waiting; quiet for 3 s means Done; OSC 9 counts too. Typing into a Waiting or Done session sets it back to Idle (try pressing a key).
  8. Background notifications, focus-awareP1M4 Sent through osascript when a session you're not looking at becomes Done or Waiting. Never for the active session while the window is focused. Shows as “Script Editor” until M11.

Not in M4 on purpose: scrollback, selection and copy, mouse, clickable links, font settings, renaming, presets (all Phase 2: see page 2). Clicking a notification can't jump to the session yet, because osascript can't report clicks (decision D5 moves it to M11).

Try it: click sessions in the sidebar, use the Simulate buttons, press a key while a red or green session is active.
