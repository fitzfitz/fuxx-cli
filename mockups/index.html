fuxx: what it will look like

A native GPU terminal for running many AI coding agents in one window. Each session gets a status ring that tells you when an agent is working, done, or waiting for you, so you can step away and get pulled back exactly when needed.

Three screens show the product as it grows. Every feature from the docs/01-Overview/11 - Features.md catalog appears on one of them, tagged with its priority and the milestone that builds it.

Illustrative mockups, not screenshots. Nothing here is built yet; the build is at M0.
