# Built-in theme attribution

The six theme files in this folder come from mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes
(https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes, `ghostty/` folder), licensed MIT. See
`LICENSE-iTerm2-Color-Schemes.txt`. That licence covers the collection; each theme's copyright
belongs to its original author. Source and licence per theme, as listed by that repository's
`licenses.yml` and `CREDITS.md` (https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/blob/master/licenses.yml),
cross-checked against the original project's own licence page:

| Theme | Original project | Licence | Checked at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catppuccin Mocha, Catppuccin Latte | https://github.com/catppuccin/palette (Catppuccin) | MIT | https://github.com/catppuccin/catppuccin/blob/main/LICENSE |
| Dracula | https://github.com/dracula/dracula-theme (draculatheme.com, zenorocha) | MIT (Dracula project); unverified for this exact file, since upstream `licenses.yml` lists Dracula as "unknown" | https://github.com/dracula/dracula-theme/blob/master/LICENSE |
| Nord | https://github.com/nordtheme/iterm2 (nordtheme, Arctic Ice Studio, Sven Greb) | MIT | https://github.com/nordtheme/nord (and `licenses.yml`) |
| Gruvbox Dark | https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox | MIT/X11 | https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox (and `licenses.yml`) |

upstream repo has no NOTICE file): the scheme is credited to folke/tokyonight.nvim, not to
