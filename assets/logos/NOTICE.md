# Provider logos

These marks identify the agent a tab runs. They are their owners' trademarks, drawn in their
own colours, and are used only to name that agent.

- `claude.svg`: the Claude mark from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.38.0 (CC0 1.0),
  filled in its brand colour `#D97757`. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic.
- `antigravity.svg`: the Antigravity mark as it ships in the Antigravity IDE
  (`antigravity-gradient-logo.svg`). Antigravity is a trademark of Google.
